## Bug Fixes

- **Compact context at works for custom endpoint models** — Pi sessions on a custom (OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible) endpoint ignored a **Settings → AI → Performance → Compact context at** value above about 115K and kept compacting at 115K. A model that doesn't set its own context window was assumed to hold 128K tokens, and Pi never compacts later than a model's window. Such models are now assumed to hold the configured size, so a 256K setting compacts at 256K. A model that sets `contextWindow` on its connection still compacts before that window, and Pi's built-in providers keep their known windows. If a model on your endpoint holds less than the setting, set its `contextWindow`, or its requests will overflow before Pi compacts. Applies to new and restarted sessions.
